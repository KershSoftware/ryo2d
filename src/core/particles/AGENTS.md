# AGENTS.md - Particles

The engine runs particles: a game describes a kind of particle as a `ParticleEffect` asset and places it with `ParticleEmitter` entities (both in the prelude); the engine steps them in its fixed step and draws them as one run per emitter, with no call from the game. The game-facing guide is the `ryo-particles` skill; measured figures and limits are in `docs/backlog/particles.md`.

| `value.rs` | `ValueRange`, `ColorRange`, `Curve` and `Gradient` (keys and a 64-entry table the step reads) |

## The engine's part

- **Effect and emitter.** `ParticleEffect` (`effect.rs`) is an asset (its store registered by `insert_registry`); `ParticleEmitter` (`emitter.rs`) places one on an entity. Its `on_insert` hook takes a slot in the `ParticleStore` resource (`store.rs`) and keeps it in the emitter (`slot`), so the step finds the pool with no lookup; the hook also records the scene whose code runs (`set_scope`) and draws the emitter's engine seed from the store's stream, so the order emitters are added in fixes their seeds. `on_discard` (taken off, put over, despawned) frees the slot and its particles. Both do nothing without a store. A freed pool keeps its arrays' room for the next emitter (last freed, first taken).
- **Three places in the frame.** `step_particles` (exclusive, `FixedSet::Particles`: after propagation and `lift_floored`, before trails) runs the game-time emitters; `step_unscaled_particles` (exclusive, `PostUpdateSet::Extract`) runs the unscaled ones on real time; the drawer joins the draw list in `draw_frame`. All are registered by the engine.
- **The budget and seeds.** `EngineBuilder::particle_budget` (default `DEFAULT_PARTICLE_BUDGET`, 20,000) through `EngineConfig::particle_budget`; the store's seed is `PARTICLE_HEADLESS_SEED` in a headless engine and from entropy otherwise, so headless runs repeat without seeding. A spawn past the budget is dropped and warned once per effect id, naming the budget and `EngineBuilder::particle_budget`; live particles are never cut short.
- **Views** (`view.rs`): `ParticlesView` (`ctx.particles()`, an exclusive system's `ParticlesView::new(world)`) and the `Particles` system parameter: `play_at` spawns `(Transform, ParticleEmitter::new(effect).once().despawn_when_finished())` (through commands for the parameter); `count` and `budget` read the store (0 and the default without one); the view's `clear` is `ParticleStore::clear_all` (emitters' `alive` catch up in their next step).
- **Holds** (`scope.rs`): the rule audio's emitters follow. The scene manager runs scene code under `particles::in_scene` (beside `audio::in_scene`), tells the store the scenes it holds (`hold_scenes`, beside audio's, the same set) and that a scene exited (`release_scene`). An emitter of a held scene is frozen, and one of no scene (added outside every scene, or outliving its scene) is frozen while any scene is held. A released emitter does not spread its first stream across what it missed.

## Storage

One `EmitterPool` per emitter (`pool.rs`), its particles field by field in spawn order (`Pool`: position, last position, velocity, age, lifetime, size, rotation, spin, colour, seed and flags in side-by-side arrays), and two sub pools (`death` and `collision`, each a `SubPool`: the particles its effect's `on_death` and `on_collision` played, the sub-effect they play, their box and their frame). `size` and `color` are start values; the effect's curves scale them as they are read. The dead leave by `Pool::retain`, which compacts in order, never `swap_remove`: particle by particle below 4,096 particles (`RUN_COMPACTION`), and above it each run of at least 8 survivors (`SHORT_RUN`) with `copy_within` on every field array, a shorter one particle by particle (the oldest die first, so a large pool that loses any particle moves nearly every survivor; a run with nothing dead before it is not touched).

## Step order

`step_particles` keeps its parameters (the emitter query among them, and the `EmitterFinished` buffer) in an `EcsSystemState` in its `Local<StepState>`, with the entity-order buffer, per-particle scratch, the despawn list and its `Surroundings`, so a steady step allocates nothing. A pass (`run_particle_step`):

1. **Gather** (`Surroundings::gather`, `forces.rs`). Reads the enabled emitters (a kept query) and, if the pass has one to step (of its kind, speed above 0, not held), copies the enabled attractors (position, type, mask, time; no heap data) into a kept buffer, and gathers the solid colliders (`gather_colliders`, its `ColliderQuery` kept) only if one of those emitters' effects collides, or one of their sub-effects (named by the effect, or still played by its pool's sub particles) does (each its compiled effect, or its asset when compiled this step), and rebuilds the collision grid from them (`ColliderGrid`, `grid.rs`; emptied in a pass that gathers none).
2. **Forget** what the store kept for effects no longer loaded (`forget_unloaded`: compiled effects and budget warnings).
3. **Order.** Each enabled game-time emitter (the default filter leaves `Disabled` out, so a disabled emitter is frozen; unscaled ones are left out of the order), in `entity_order`, whose effect is loaded and which is not held or at speed 0, is stepped by `Pass::step_emitter`, which the unscaled pass shares. An effect not in `Assets` yet (a queued load) is skipped with no warning, and its emitter has no cycle until the step it lands. A held emitter (below), or one at speed 0, is skipped too, keeping its `teleported` mark and its controls for the step that runs it. `dt` is the fixed timestep times its speed (0 to 100).
4. **`Pass::step_emitter`**, for one emitter:
   1. A `despawn_when_finished` emitter already reported finished, still finished at the start of this step, goes on the despawn list instead of stepping.
   2. The effect is compiled once per `(AssetId, version)` (`Compiled`: `sanitized()`, angles in radians); one with `problems()` is warned about once per version, naming its asset path or "an effect added in code". Sub-effects compile with it (`compile_subs`, below). A new effect (the emitter's `effect` field set) starts the emission over.
   3. Its seed, at its first step with an effect: `with_seed`, else the effect's `seed`, else its engine seed. Each emitter draws from its own `ParticleRng`, so another emitter, entity or resource never changes its particles.
   4. Controls: the emitter's methods only set its fields (`playing`, `finish`, and `pending`: `clear`, `restart`, `burst`), and the step does them. `clear` and `restart` empty the pool (off the live count); `restart`, or `playing` set again on a pool whose one cycle `ended`, starts the cycle at time 0 (no start delay) with the bursts re-armed. A pending `burst` is added to what the effect emits, within `max_particles` and the budget, whether or not it plays (the rest is dropped).
   5. Position: its `GlobalTransform` translation plus `DrawLift.lift`. A teleport is `skip_interpolation`, which marks the emitter (its crate-internal `teleported`, honoured and cleared here) whether or not the entity is `Interpolated` and whenever it is called (the step's snapshot clears `PreviousGlobalTransform.skip` before particles run, so a call between steps would otherwise be lost), or a `PreviousGlobalTransform.skip` set within the step. The stream spreads from the place it stood last step unless it was not stepped in the previous step (just spawned, re-enabled, released from a hold) or it teleported.
   6. Local space (`space.rs`): a `Local` effect's particles live in the emitter's frame (`LocalFrame`: its position as above, and its axes turned and mirrored but never stretched, `rigid_axes`). `space::settle` moves live particles between spaces when a new version changes `space`, so they stay where they were.
   7. Prewarm (`Stepper::prewarm`): a looping run (`prewarm` on a looping effect, the emitter not `once`; `problems` reports `prewarm:` on an effect that does not loop and `sanitized` turns it off) whose `pool.time` is still `None` as it plays (its first step, or its first since a new effect; not after `restart`) first runs `min(duration, 10)` s from cycle time 0 (its start delay skipped), standing where it is, in steps of its `dt` (at most `MAX_PREWARM_STEPS`, 600, longer ones past that), its collisions found then dropped (`Surroundings::forget_told_since`), so it writes no `ParticleCollision`; its sub-effects play in it as in any step (sub spawns write no message, so a prewarmed effect starts with its deaths' and collisions' particles already out); no `EmitterFinished` is looked for until its real step, which then spreads from where it stands.
   8. Emission (`emission`, in `Stepper::particles`): the cycle time starts at `-start_delay`; the rate adds to a fractional carry (spawning `floor(carry + 1e-4)`), over the step's part inside the cycle for one cycle and on through every cycle when looping; each burst fires every time due by the step's end (with a 1e-4 s tolerance, as step times summed in floats never land on a burst exactly), each repeat `interval` apart, as many as its `cycles` (0: all) and only before the cycle ends, so a hitch owes and fires them all. A one-cycle run (an effect that does not loop, or an emitter made `once`) ends with its cycle: the pool's `ended` is set and the emitter's `playing` cleared; a looping one starts its bursts over (at most 1,000 cycles a step). A burst count is rounded, below 0 spawning nothing. Spawns are capped by `max_particles` against the pool's count, then by the store's budget; the carry past a cap is dropped, not saved up. `rate_per_distance` adds to the same carry the distance moved in the step (none across a teleport or in a first step), weighted by the step's part inside the cycle as the rate is.
   9. Spawning: `shape_point` places each uniformly over the shape's area (the band to its edge by `thickness`; the outline by length at 0), the arc from 0 (right) towards 90 degrees (down), through the emitter's linear part, so a zero scale puts every point at the centre. Directions are `direction_deg ± spread_deg / 2`, or outward from the shape's centre (any direction at the centre), through the emitter's unit axes (`unit_axes`: turned and mirrored, never stretched), so scale moves the shape only and never particle size or speed. A `World` effect's particles start with `inherit_velocity` times the emitter's velocity (`Frame::inherited`: its move over `dt`, zero when the stream does not spread, so after a teleport, in a first step or once re-enabled, and when not finite); a `World` stream's `n` spawns are placed at `(i + 1) / n` of the way from where the emitter stood in the step before, each having lived the rest of the step (its partial step integrated like any other), all where it stands, aged 0, when it does not spread. A `Local` effect's are born at the frame's origin (offset by the shape through its linear part, aged along the step but not spread along the move), with none of its velocity.
   10. Every particle ages (`advance`); one whose age reaches its lifetime dies where it stood as the step began (no last move, which could carry it past a wall uncollided). The rest take the attractors' pull, then gravity, then the effect's own forces (`Own::force`: radial and tangential acceleration, each drawn per particle from a hash of its `seed` with a different salt (`ValueRange::at`), no storage; tangential is `(out.y, -out.x)`, counter-clockwise on screen with +y down; and the `noise` force: `noise_force(emitter seed folded to u32, position, age * speed, frequency) * strength`), all about the emitter's current position, or the origin of a `Local` effect's frame, then drag (`v *= max(1 - drag dt, 0)`), then its speed curve's change (`speed_over_life(t_now) / speed_over_life(t_before)`, unchanged when the earlier value is 0 or the ratio not finite; a constant curve is skipped), spins, moves from where it was (`px`, `py` kept for drawing between steps), and meets the colliders. Each emitter takes the attractors whose mask is 0 or shares a bit with its effect's `layer`, and, if its effect has a `collision`, marks the gathered colliders it can hit (`can_hit`: its own entity's left out, and those off its mask); each particle meets those of them the grid finds near its move (Collisions, below). A stuck one (`STUCK`) only ages: no forces, movement or spin, its `px`/`py` following so it draws still; it is held by its effect's `Stick`, so one whose effect no longer sticks (`Compiled::sticks`, false after a reload that drops the collision or changes the response) is released, its flag cleared, and moves again. A `Local` effect's particles act only under their own forces in the frame: `Surroundings::acting` gives them no attractors, `problems` reports `"collision: a Local effect does not collide"` and `sanitized` drops the collision. The box round the survivors' current and previous positions is kept (`bounds`, a `Local` pool's in the world, round this step's frame and the last's).
   11. Sub-emitters (`sub.rs`; `SubEmitter`, an effect's `on_death` and `on_collision`): the step compiles each sub-effect as any effect (`compile_subs`: those the effect names and those its sub pools still play, by id and version, so a sub-effect's reload applies; one not loaded yet spawns nothing, with no warning), and warns once per compiled version about a sub-effect with sub-emitters of its own (`"Played ... as a sub-effect: on_death: a sub-effect's own sub-emitters are ignored"`, `Compiled::warned_nested`): sub-effects go one level deep. `advance` gives the main pool's deaths and telling contacts to `Triggers` (kept in the step's scratch, armed only for a sub-effect that is compiled): `on_death` fires where a particle's life ended (where it stood as the step began) or where a `Die` met the surface; `on_collision` on each contact that would tell of itself (`Die`, `Stick`, a `Bounce` entry at `MIN_BOUNCE_EVENT_SPEED` or above, an `Event` entry) whether or not events are on (`Acting::collide` returns it as `Met::hit`): on the surface, the particle's radius out (as `Stick` places it), with its velocity before the response. A `Die` contact plays both. After the main pool steps, `Stepper::subs` steps each sub pool: it plays its parent's sub-effect once that is compiled (one still loading is not adopted, so the particles it has go on by the effect they play; and once the parent names none, the last it had; its particles go once that effect is unloaded), each trigger spawns `count` particles (rounded, drawn from the emitter's own stream, as are their values) with the sub-effect's shape (through the emitter's linear part), direction (through its unit axes) and start values, at the trigger in the sub-effect's space (a `Local` sub-effect's in the emitter's frame), plus `inherit_velocity` times the particle's velocity, within the sub-effect's `max_particles` and the budget (warned per sub-effect); its particles then step by the sub-effect (motion, life, forces about the emitter, collisions and attractors on its layer), those born in the step not moving, and leave no triggers. The sub-effect's emission, seed and own `inherit_velocity` play no part. Sub particles count in the live count and the emitter's `alive`, are removed by `clear` and `restart`, and go with the emitter's pool.
   12. `alive` and `playing` are written to the emitter only when they change. An emitter is finished when not playing with nothing alive (its sub-effects' particles too) and no burst pending (`is_finished`, read from its fields). Its `finish` is `Armed` (new, `play`, `restart`), `Idle` (made `stopped`: no report before it plays, so one driven only by `burst` never reports or despawns) or `Reported`: the step that finds an armed emitter finished writes one `EmitterFinished` and marks it reported.
5. **After the pass.** The pass's `ParticleCollision`s (`emitter_entity` the emitter's entity) are written as one batch (`tell_collisions`). The game-time pass then advances turbulence attractors' time by the fixed timestep, only when it had an emitter to step. The emitters pushed to the despawn list are despawned (`try_despawn`, children with it), so a game-time emitter's entity still exists for the scenes' next `update` after its finish; an unscaled emitter that takes several steps in a frame can finish and despawn in one frame.

## Unscaled emitters

`step_unscaled_particles` (`PostUpdateSet::Extract`, its own `Local<StepState>`): each frame (the store's `frame` counter), every enabled `unscaled` emitter, in `entity_order`, adds `time.real` delta times its speed to its pool's `clock` (a `Duration`) and takes a step of the fixed step's length per step's worth, at most `MAX_UNSCALED_STEPS` (8; whole steps past them are dropped, the remainder kept). Holds, a paused clock and hitstop do not apply. Its stream spreads from its last step's place if it ran in the frame before (`last_step` holds the frame for these pools). `pool.fraction` (clock over step length, below 1) is kept for the drawer. With no unscaled emitters the pass only counts the frame.

## Draw

`ParticleDrawer` (`draw.rs`), an `Engine` field beside the entity drawer, keeps its `QueryState` and order buffer between frames; `draw_frame` calls `collect` just after the scenes' sprites join the draw list, under `render:particles`. Every enabled emitter (the default filter hides a `Disabled` one's particles), in `entity_order`, whose pool has particles and a compiled effect, and whose box (`bounds` grown by `Compiled::largest_size`: the largest start size times the size curve's largest value) meets a camera's view on its `RenderLayers` (else the default), pushes one `DrawRun` (`DrawList::push_run`: `DrawKind::Sprite` at the emitter's `z()`, else the effect's `z`; the effect's `lit`, `blend` and `filter`; `SpriteShading::Effect` when it has a sprite effect), then one for each of its sub pools with particles, right after it (just over it) at the same z, with the sub-effect's look and culled by the sub pool's own box (grown by the sub-effect's `largest_size`). Its texture comes from `texture_of`, as the scenes' sprites' do (the checker while the image is not loaded in debug builds, nothing in release ones).

Its instances (`instance_for`) are its particles in spawn order, the newest on top (reversed for the effect's `DrawOrder::OldestOnTop`): `px`/`py` blended toward `x`/`y` by the frame's `overstep_fraction` (an unscaled pool by its own `fraction`; a game-time pool not stepped in the store's latest step, held or at speed 0, draws where it is, `drawn_fraction`, or it would sway with the frame's fraction), a `Local` pool's (`pool.local`, or a sub pool's `local`, set) then placed through its emitter's frame as entities draw (`LocalFrame::drawn`: `render_global` by the frame's fraction plus its `DrawLift`, read by a kept-query `get` for that emitter only), the start size times the size curve and the start colour times the gradient channel by channel at `age / lifetime`, its rotation (turned with a `Local` pool's frame), and the effect's sprite effect and emission as `BatchCollector::add_shaded` folds them. The particles submitted count in `DebugStats::sprite_count`; the F1 stats' `Particles: {live} / {budget}` row reads the store.

## Effect files

`.particles.ron`: `ParticleEffectLoader` (`asset/loaders/particles.rs`, registered by `insert_registry`) reads one effect into a private file struct, field per field with `ParticleEffect::new`'s defaults (`image` required, unknown fields refused, `IMPLICIT_SOME`), and builds the effect. Colours are `"#rrggbb"`, `"#rrggbbaa"` (`ui`'s `parse_hex`) or `(r:, g:, b:, a:)`, `a` 255 when left out, in `color`, colour pairs and gradient keys; a `SpriteEffect`'s colours are its plain serde fields. Other forms are the engine types' own serde (`Burst`, `EmitShape` with `arc_deg` and `thickness` optional, `Flipbook`, `NoiseForce`, `Align`, `CollisionConfig` through `CollisionFile`, its `Bounce(0.6)` restitution refused outside 0 to 1). A malformed `ValueRange` or colour range says what was expected (their `Deserialize` is a visitor, not an untagged enum). A RON error keeps `line L, column C`; curve and gradient keys (in order, `t` within 0 to 1, finite values, which `Curve` and `Gradient` would otherwise fix up) are checked before anything loads; then `image` and the sub-effects load as dependencies; then `ParticleEffect::problems` names every bad field (`AssetError::decode`, joined by "; "). A file naming a sub-effect that has sub-emitters of its own loads with a warning (the loader's `check`, again whenever one reloads; the step still warns once per compiled version when it plays it).

Reloads need nothing in the step: compiled effects follow versions, so over-life values, forces, look and collisions apply to live particles at the next step, spawn values (and a lower `max_particles`) to spawns, and an emitter keeps its cycle time and burst schedule (`pool.fired` grows with the bursts). A failed reload keeps the last good effect (`ReloadFailed`, warned once by the registry). A reload that lowers `max_particles` below the live count kills nothing: `room` is 0 until enough die.

## On a floor

An emitter on a storey (with a `Floor`, or below an entity with one) emits where it draws: lifted by its `DrawLift`, as the tile map's `lift_floored` last found it (each fixed step after propagation, before particles), so an emitter spawned and fired in one step fires from its lifted point. Its particles then fly, collide and are attracted in ground space: the colliders and attractors are where their entities stand, so a lifted emitter's sparks pass through the storey's drawn surface and meet the colliders a storey's height below (a recorded limit).

## Gotchas

- **One-shots** - `despawn_when_finished` (as `ParticlesView::play_at` sets it) despawns an emitter in the step after its finish; any other finished emitter stays until the game despawns it. `EmitterFinished` is sent in the step it finishes, so a game-time emitter's entity is there for the next `update`; an unscaled one can finish and despawn within one frame.
- **`burst` on a `stopped()` emitter** - it spawns, but `Finish::Idle` means no `EmitterFinished` and no despawn: only `play` or `restart` arm it.
- **Teleports** - `skip_interpolation` marks the emitter, so it never streaks, `Interpolated` or not.
- **Local effects** - a `Local` effect ignores `inherit_velocity`, collisions and attractors, and its stream does not spread along a move.
- **Layer masks need effect layers** - an attractor's `with_layer_mask(mask)` only affects particles whose effect's `layer` shares a bit with it. Effects default to layer 0, which only unmasked attractors (mask 0) affect. `layer` is not a render layer: the drawer reads the entity's `RenderLayers`.
- **Disabled emitters** - a `Disabled` emitter is frozen (its particles neither move nor age) and its particles are not drawn.
- **Empty ranges** - a min above its max (or a zero spread or zero-size shape) samples the min; a min that is not a number samples 0. Nothing panics.
- **Randomness** - each emitter draws from its own `ParticleRng`, written in the module (with the noise), so particles repeat per engine version whatever `rand` does; seeds as under the step above.

## Collisions

An effect's `collision` (`ParticleEffect::with_collision(CollisionConfig)`) makes the step collide its particles with the world (`forces.rs`, the geometry in `contact.rs`, the grid in `grid.rs`, the config types in `collision.rs`):

- **Colliders** - entities with `GlobalTransform` + `Collider`, gathered once per pass and only when an emitter it steps (or a sub-effect of one) collides. Trigger colliders and `Disabled` entities are skipped. No `RigidBody` is needed. Geometry is the collider as physics places it (its entity's world transform, then its `offset` and `rotation`):
  - a circle is a circle;
  - a `Segment`, `Polyline` or `Segments` collider is its edges (`ColliderGeometry::Edges`, world-space edges in the pass's `edges` list, with their box for a quick reject): a particle meets an edge whose nearest point is within its radius, its normal the edge's turned to the side the particle is on (from the edge's end at an end), or an edge its move crossed this step, at the crossing, its normal to the side it came from (`edge_contact`; a crossing is met before any edge it merely ends near, the earliest first, else the nearest). A tile map's colliders are such edges, a chunk's outlines packed in one collider whose box is mostly air: by box, particles bounced off the air in it (`particles_meet_a_tile_part_s_edges_not_its_box`). The crossing keeps a fast particle from passing through an edge, which has no inside to catch it (`a_particle_crossing_an_edge_within_an_update_still_bounces`). Only a tile part's loops bound something solid: a `TilePart` that is not one-way, whose `Segments` (or, for a chunk of one loop, closed `Polyline`) physics winds clockwise on screen round its ground, holes included, so the ground is on the same side of every edge. They are stored turned round so each edge's left normal points out (unless the placement mirrors them), with `solid: Some(reach)`, `reach` half the cell of the `TileColliders` the part is a child of (`solid_contact`). A move into the ground across an edge is met where it crossed in; a move out is not met. Otherwise the part's nearest point to the particle decides, locally: within `reach` on its inner side (by the edge's normal, or at a corner the sum of its edges' normals) the particle is in the ground and goes out there, so landing dust spawned round the feet ends on top (`a_particle_starting_just_inside_tile_ground_ends_on_top_of_it`); on its outer side within the radius it touches. Local, because the loops round a place may lie in several parts: a room carved from a wall has its own loop, likely in another chunk's part, and a parity test over one part's edges judged every particle in such a room inside the wall mass and moved it to the mass's far edge each step (`a_particle_in_a_room_whose_wall_s_outline_lies_in_another_part_drifts_untouched`). The half-cell bound keeps a part's loop from claiming air a thinner wall away; ground deeper than that is not judged, and a particle in a thin floor leaves through the nearer face. A hole alone in its chunk, a closed polyline wound as a hole's, takes the tile rule too (`a_particle_in_a_room_alone_in_its_part_bounces_off_its_walls`). Every other collider's edges (a segment, any polyline, closed or not, a one-way run, a game's own `Segments`) are two-sided, as physics treats them: the side is the one the particle came from (`particles_stay_in_a_game_s_closed_polyline_arena_and_bounce`);
  - every other shape is its world bounds (`Collider::world_bounds`), a box.
- **Filtering** - a collider counts only if `collider.layer & config.collision_mask != 0`. A tile map storey's edge (the parts of a `TileColliders` whose crate-internal `surface` is false, as the map sets it on the grid that keeps characters on a raised floor; read in the same lookup as the part's cell) is no surface, and never counts (`a_raised_floor_s_edge_is_no_surface_for_particles`). The collider on a particle's own emitter entity is always ignored (particles spawn inside it). Each particle is a circle of `config.radius`. `Bounce` resolves every overlapping collider in turn (so a particle sliding on a floor still hits a wall gathered after it) but sends at most one event per step (a corner hit is one event); `Die`, `Stick` and `Event` stop at the first contact. Each collider is resolved once per step, so a particle squeezed between nearly opposing colliders can end a step overlapping one of them; the next step corrects it, and a gap narrower than the particle can't be resolved at all.
- **Responses** - `Die` removes the particle. `Bounce` reflects velocity along the contact normal with the restitution, then pushes the particle just outside the surface. `Stick` puts the particle on the surface, its radius out from the contact point along the normal (as `Bounce` places it: a fast particle that crossed an edge sticks where it crossed, not where its move ended inside, `a_fast_stick_particle_sticks_on_the_surface_it_crossed`), zeroes velocity and sets the private `stuck` flag: no more forces or movement, but it still ages, fades and dies (until a reload leaves its effect not sticking, which releases it). `Event` passes through.
- **Messages** - opt-in with `CollisionConfig::with_events()`: `Die` and `Stick` write one `ParticleCollision` message (read with `ctx.messages::<ParticleCollision>()`). `Bounce` emits only on contact entry (the particle touched no collider last step) and only when the impact speed along the normal is at least `MIN_BOUNCE_EVENT_SPEED` (20 px/s, also in the prelude). A particle resting on a surface stays touching, so it emits nothing more, whatever the gravity or dt. `CollisionResponse::Event` always emits on entry. Both entry checks use the private `touching` flag (not-touching to touching), which is per particle, not per collider: moving straight from one collider into an overlapping or adjacent one (or sliding along a floor into a wall) doesn't notify again. Events carry the emitter and collider entities, the particle position, the surface normal and the velocity before the response.
- **Cost** - a uniform grid (`grid.rs`), so cost follows the colliders near each particle. A pass that gathers colliders rebuilds it into the buffers it keeps (`ColliderGrid::rebuild`): circles and boxes go in the cells their box covers, an edges collider's edges each in the cells its own box covers (edges of no length or not numbers, which no contact reads, nowhere), and each edge notes its collider. A cell is `clamp(2 × mean edge length, 16, 256)` world units wide, or the mean circle and box side when there are no edges, doubled until the grid holds at most 65,536 cells. A collider or edge with bounds that are not numbers, or covering more than 1,024 cells, goes in a list every query finds, as every collider was met before. A particle's query (`query_move`) is its move's box grown by its radius or the largest gathered reach (a tile part's half cell), whichever is larger, plus half a unit and 1/65,536 of its coordinates' size (so the 1e-3 within which `solid_contact` adds a corner's normals, and rounding, never leave out an edge a contact reads); a move, radius or box that is not all numbers finds everything. It lists the colliders (gather order, no repeats, edges colliders by their found edges) and edges (ascending) in the cells it covers (`Candidates`, kept), and each collider it can hit is met in gather order over only its found edges (`ParticleCollider::contact_among`, in `edge_contact` and `solid_contact` alike), which gives exactly the contact over all of its edges: every edge that could take part (crossed, within the radius, the nearest within the reach, or a corner beside it) is found, and none of the rest could change the contact. A bounce that pushes the particle out past the move its candidates were found for finds them again for the move to where it now is, carrying on after the collider just met, so every collider is still met at the particle's current place in gather order. `the_grid_finds_what_brute_force_finds` (10,000 seeded moves at radius 2 and 6 over a busy level, `testing::busy_level`: each collider's contact bit for bit), `responses_over_a_busy_level_match_brute_force` (every response through the grid and through a grid that finds everything, `rebuild_whole`) and `a_corner_a_hair_past_the_reach_is_still_read` hold it to testing everything.
- **Tunnelling** - collisions are checked at particle positions once per step, so very fast particles can pass through thin boxes and circles. Use thicker colliders or lower speeds. Edges also catch a move that crossed them.
