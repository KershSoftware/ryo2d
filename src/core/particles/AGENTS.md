# AGENTS.md - Particle System

The engine runs particles: a game describes a kind of particle as a `ParticleEffect` asset and places it with `ParticleEmitter` entities (both in the prelude); the engine steps them in its fixed step and draws them as one run per emitter, with no call from the game.

## The engine-driven system

- **Effect and emitter.** `ParticleEffect` (`effect.rs`) is an asset (its store registered by `insert_registry`); `ParticleEmitter` (`emitter.rs`) places one on an entity. Its `on_insert` hook takes a slot in the `ParticleStore` resource (`store.rs`) and keeps it in the emitter (`slot`), so the step finds the pool with no lookup; the hook also records the scene whose code runs (`set_scope`) and draws the emitter's engine seed from the store's stream, so the order emitters are added in fixes their seeds. `on_discard` (taken off, put over, despawned) frees the slot and its particles. Both do nothing without a store. A freed pool keeps its arrays' room for the next emitter (last freed, first taken).
- **Storage** (`pool.rs`): one `EmitterPool` per emitter, its particles field by field in spawn order (`Pool`). The dead leave by `Pool::retain`, which compacts in order, never `swap_remove`. `size` and `color` are start values; the effect's curves scale them as they are read.
- **The step** (`step.rs`, `step_particles`, exclusive, `FixedSet::Particles`: after propagation and `lift_floored`, before trails). Its parameters (the emitter query among them, and the `EmitterFinished` buffer) live in an `EcsSystemState` kept in its `Local<StepState>`, with the entity-order buffer, per-particle scratch, the despawn list and its `Surroundings` (`forces.rs`: the attractors and colliders it gathers, with their kept queries), so a steady step allocates nothing. Each enabled game-time emitter (the default filter leaves `Disabled` out, so a disabled emitter is frozen; unscaled ones are left out of the order) whose effect is loaded, in `entity_order`, is stepped by `Pass::step_emitter`, which the unscaled pass shares:
  - An effect not in `Assets` yet (a queued load) is skipped with no warning, and its emitter has no cycle until the step it lands. A held emitter (below), or one at speed 0, is skipped too, keeping its `teleported` mark and its controls for the step that runs it.
  - Controls first: the emitter's methods only set its fields (`playing`, `finish`, and `pending`: `clear`, `restart`, `burst`), and the step does them. `clear` and `restart` empty the pool (off the live count); `restart`, or `playing` set again on a pool whose one cycle `ended`, starts the cycle at time 0 (no start delay) with the bursts re-armed. A pending `burst` is added to what the effect emits, within `max_particles` and the budget, whether or not it plays (the rest is dropped).
  - The effect is compiled once per `(AssetId, version)` (`Compiled`: `sanitized()`, angles in radians); one with `problems()` is warned about once per version, naming its asset path or "an effect added in code". Compiled effects of assets no longer loaded are dropped each step.
  - Its seed, at its first step with an effect: `with_seed`, else the effect's `seed`, else its engine seed. Each emitter draws from its own `ParticleRng`, so another emitter, entity or resource never changes its particles.
  - Position: its `GlobalTransform` translation plus `DrawLift.lift`. `dt` is the fixed timestep times its speed (0 to 100).
  - Emission (`emission`): the cycle time starts at `-start_delay`; the rate adds to a fractional carry (spawning `floor(carry + 1e-4)`), over the step's part inside the cycle for one cycle and on through every cycle when looping; each burst fires every time due by the step's end (with a 1e-4 s tolerance, as step times summed in floats never land on a burst exactly), each repeat `interval` apart, as many as its `cycles` (0: all) and only before the cycle ends, so a hitch owes and fires them all. A one-cycle run (an effect that does not loop, or an emitter made `once`) ends with its cycle: the pool's `ended` is set and the emitter's `playing` cleared; a looping one starts its bursts over (at most 1,000 cycles a step). A burst count is rounded, below 0 spawning nothing. Spawns are capped by `max_particles` against the pool's count, then by the store's budget: a spawn past the budget is dropped and warned once per effect id, naming the budget and `EngineBuilder::particle_budget`. The carry past a cap is dropped, not saved up. `rate_per_distance` adds to the same carry the distance moved in the step (`from` to `to` below, so none across a teleport or in a first step), weighted by the step's part inside the cycle as the rate is; its spawns spread along the move with the rate's (`Stepper::particles` runs emission, caps, spawns and `advance` for one step).
  - Prewarm (`Stepper::prewarm`): a looping run (`prewarm` on a looping effect, the emitter not `once`; `problems` reports `prewarm:` on an effect that does not loop and `sanitized` turns it off) whose `pool.time` is still `None` as it plays (its first step, or its first since a new effect; not after `restart`) first runs `min(duration, 10)` s from cycle time 0 (its start delay skipped), standing where it is, in steps of its `dt` (at most `MAX_PREWARM_STEPS`, 600, longer ones past that), its collisions found then dropped (`Acting::forget_told_since`), so it writes no `ParticleCollision`; no `EmitterFinished` is looked for until its real step, which then spreads from where it stands.
  - Spawn points (`shape_point`): uniform over the shape's area (the band to its edge by `thickness`; the outline by length at 0), the arc from 0 (right) towards 90 degrees (down), through the emitter's linear part, so a zero scale puts every point at the centre. Directions are `direction_deg ± spread_deg / 2`, or outward from the shape's centre (any direction at the centre), through the emitter's unit axes (`unit_axes`: turned and mirrored, never stretched), so scale moves the shape only and never particle size or speed. A `World` effect's particles start with `inherit_velocity` times the emitter's velocity (`Frame::inherited`: its move over `dt`, zero when the stream does not spread, so after a teleport, in a first step or once re-enabled, and when not finite).
  - Local space (`space.rs`): a `Local` effect's particles live in the emitter's frame (`LocalFrame`: its position as above, and its axes turned and mirrored but never stretched, `rigid_axes`), born at its origin (offset by the shape through its linear part, aged along the step but not spread along the move) with none of its velocity; gravity, drag and the curves act in the frame, and nothing else acts on them: `Surroundings::acting` gives them no attractors, `problems` reports `"collision: a Local effect does not collide"` and `sanitized` drops the collision. The pool keeps the frame of its last step (`pool.local`, `None` for a `World` effect's); its box is kept in the world, round the particles in this step's frame and the last's. `space::settle` moves live particles between spaces when a new version changes `space`, so they stay where they were.
  - The stream spreads along the move: the step's `n` spawns are placed at `(i + 1) / n` of the way from where the emitter stood in the step before, each having lived the rest of the step (its partial step integrated like any other), unless it was not stepped in the previous step (just spawned, re-enabled, released from a hold) or it was teleported: then all spawn where it stands, aged 0. A teleport is `skip_interpolation`, which marks the emitter (its crate-internal `teleported`, honoured and cleared by its next step) whether or not the entity is `Interpolated` and whenever it is called (the step's snapshot clears `PreviousGlobalTransform.skip` before particles run, so a call between steps would otherwise be lost), or a `PreviousGlobalTransform.skip` set within the step.
  - Every particle ages, takes the attractors' pull, then gravity, then drag (`v *= max(1 - drag dt, 0)`), then its speed curve's change (`speed_over_life(t_now) / speed_over_life(t_before)`, unchanged when the earlier value is 0 or the ratio not finite; a constant curve is skipped), spins, moves from where it was (`px`, `py` kept for drawing between steps), and, unless it dies of age in this step, meets the colliders. A stuck one (`STUCK`) only ages: no forces, movement or spin, its `px`/`py` following so it draws still. A particle dies at `age >= lifetime`; the box round the survivors' current and previous positions is kept (`bounds`), and `alive` and `playing` are written to the emitter only when they change.
  - Attractors and collisions (`forces.rs`, both passes): before a pass, `Surroundings::gather` reads the enabled emitters (a kept query) and, if the pass has one to step (of its kind, speed above 0, not held), copies the enabled attractors (position, type, mask, time; no heap data) into a kept buffer, and gathers the solid colliders (`gather_colliders`, its `ColliderQuery` kept) only if one of those emitters' effects collides (its compiled effect, or its asset when compiled this step). Each emitter then takes the attractors whose mask is 0 or shares a bit with its effect's `layer`, and, if its effect has a `collision`, the colliders it can hit (`candidate_colliders`, in gather order, its own entity's left out). Contacts, responses, events and the `stuck` and `touching` rules are below under Collisions, on the pool's `STUCK` and `TOUCHING` flag bits. The pass's `ParticleCollision`s (`emitter_entity` the emitter's entity) are written as one batch after it. The game-time pass then advances turbulence attractors' time by the fixed timestep, only when it had an emitter to step.
  - Finishing: an emitter is finished when not playing with nothing alive and no burst pending (`is_finished`, read from its fields). Its `finish` is `Armed` (new, `play`, `restart`), `Idle` (made `stopped`: no report before it plays) or `Reported`: the step that finds an armed emitter finished writes one `EmitterFinished` and marks it reported. A `despawn_when_finished` emitter still reported and finished at the start of its next step is pushed to the despawn list instead of stepping, and despawned (`try_despawn`, children with it) after the loop, so the message's entity still exists for the scenes' next `update`.
- **Unscaled emitters** (`step_unscaled_particles`, exclusive, `PostUpdateSet::Extract`, its own `Local<StepState>`): each frame (the store's `frame` counter), every enabled `unscaled` emitter, in `entity_order`, adds `time.real` delta times its speed to its pool's `clock` (a `Duration`) and takes a step of the fixed step's length per step's worth, at most `MAX_UNSCALED_STEPS` (8; whole steps past them are dropped, the remainder kept). Holds, a paused clock and hitstop do not apply. Its stream spreads from its last step's place if it ran in the frame before (`last_step` holds the frame for these pools). `pool.fraction` (clock over step length, below 1) is kept for the drawer. With no unscaled emitters the pass only counts the frame.
- **Drawing** (`draw.rs`): `ParticleDrawer`, an `Engine` field beside the entity drawer, keeps its `QueryState` and order buffer between frames; `draw_frame` calls `collect` just after the scenes' sprites join the draw list, under `render:particles`. Every enabled emitter (the default filter hides a `Disabled` one's particles), in `entity_order`, whose pool has particles and a compiled effect, and whose box (`bounds` grown by `Compiled::largest_size`: the largest start size times the size curve's largest value) meets a camera's view on its `RenderLayers` (else the default), pushes one `DrawRun` (`DrawList::push_run`: `DrawKind::Sprite` at the emitter's `z()`, else the effect's `z`; the effect's `lit`, `blend` and `filter`; `SpriteShading::Effect` when it has a sprite effect). Its texture comes from `texture_of`, as the scenes' sprites' do (the checker while the image is not loaded in debug builds, nothing in release ones). Its instances (`instance_for`) are its particles in spawn order, the newest on top (the effect's `draw_order` is not read yet): `px`/`py` blended toward `x`/`y` by the frame's `overstep_fraction` (an unscaled pool by its own `fraction`; a game-time pool not stepped in the store's latest step, held or at speed 0, draws where it is, `drawn_fraction`, or it would sway with the frame's fraction), a `Local` pool's (`pool.local` set) then placed through its emitter's frame as entities draw (`LocalFrame::drawn`: `render_global` by the frame's fraction plus its `DrawLift`, read by a kept-query `get` for that emitter only), the start size times the size curve and the start colour times the gradient channel by channel at `age / lifetime`, its rotation (turned with a `Local` pool's frame), and the effect's sprite effect and emission as `BatchCollector::add_shaded` folds them. The particles submitted count in `DebugStats::sprite_count`; the F1 stats' `Particles: {live} / {budget}` row reads the store.
- **Views** (`view.rs`): `ParticlesView` (`ctx.particles()`, an exclusive system's `ParticlesView::new(world)`) and the `Particles` system parameter: `play_at` spawns `(Transform, ParticleEmitter::new(effect).once().despawn_when_finished())` (through commands for the parameter); `count` and `budget` read the store (0 and the default without one); the view's `clear` is `ParticleStore::clear_all` (emitters' `alive` catch up in their next step).
- **Holds** (`scope.rs`): the rule audio's emitters follow. The scene manager runs scene code under `particles::in_scene` (beside `audio::in_scene`), tells the store the scenes it holds (`hold_scenes`, beside audio's, the same set) and that a scene exited (`release_scene`). An emitter of a held scene is frozen, and one of no scene (added outside every scene, or outliving its scene) is frozen while any scene is held. A released emitter does not spread its first stream across what it missed.
- **The budget and seeds**: `EngineBuilder::particle_budget` (default `DEFAULT_PARTICLE_BUDGET`, 20,000) through `EngineConfig::particle_budget`; the store's seed is `PARTICLE_HEADLESS_SEED` in a headless engine and from entropy otherwise, so headless runs repeat without seeding.
- **Tests**: `testing.rs` (a world with the stores and a schedule of propagation, `lift_floored` and the step; `frame` runs the unscaled pass on a real delta; `particles` reads a pool (sizes and colours as drawn, places in its space), with its `stuck` and `touching` flags; `edit` changes a pool as a test places its particles; `finished` and `collisions` the `EmitterFinished` and `ParticleCollision` messages so far; `drawn` places a pool's particles as the drawer does, through a `Local` pool's frame), `tests/step.rs`, `tests/control.rs`, `tests/collisions.rs`, `tests/attractors.rs` (collisions and attractor layers) and `tests/emission.rs` (rate over distance, inherited velocity, local space, prewarm); `draw.rs`'s own (`instance_for`, `drawn_fraction`) and `space.rs`'s; `tests/e2e/particles.rs` drives a headless engine (`HeadlessEngine::step_real` for real deltas, `ryo2d::testing::particle_positions`, in the world (a `Local` pool's through its last step's frame), `HeadlessEngine::debug_stats` for what was submitted, pixels for what was drawn); `examples/demos/particles/tests/pages.rs` runs every demo page. Particle warnings go to the dev log under the module "Particles"; tests that count them or expect none hold `testing::log_lock`.

## On a floor

An emitter on a storey (with a `Floor`, or below an entity with one) emits where it draws: lifted by its `DrawLift`, as the tile map's `lift_floored` last found it (each fixed step after propagation, before particles). Its particles then fly, collide and are attracted in ground space: the colliders and attractors are where their entities stand, so a lifted emitter's sparks pass through the storey's drawn surface and meet the colliders a storey's height below (a recorded limit).

## Gotchas

- **One-shots** - `despawn_when_finished` (as `ParticlesView::play_at` sets it) despawns an emitter in the step after its finish; any other finished emitter stays until the game despawns it.
- **Layer masks need effect layers** - an attractor's `with_layer_mask(mask)` only affects particles whose effect's `layer` shares a bit with it. Effects default to layer 0, which only unmasked attractors (mask 0) affect.
- **Disabled emitters** - a `Disabled` emitter is frozen (its particles neither move nor age) and its particles are not drawn.
- **Empty ranges** - a min above its max (or a zero spread or zero-size shape) samples the min; a min that is not a number samples 0. Nothing panics.
- **Randomness** - each emitter draws from its own `ParticleRng`, written in the module (with the noise), so particles repeat per engine version whatever `rand` does; seeds as under the step above.

## Collisions

An effect's `collision` (`ParticleEffect::with_collision(CollisionConfig)`) makes the step collide its particles with the world (`forces.rs`, the geometry in `contact.rs`, config types in `collision.rs`); "update" below is a step:

- **Colliders** - entities with `GlobalTransform` + `Collider`, gathered once per update and only when some emitter has a collision config. Trigger colliders and `Disabled` entities are skipped. No `RigidBody` is needed. Geometry is the collider as physics places it (its entity's world transform, then its `offset` and `rotation`):
  - a circle is a circle;
  - a `Segment`, `Polyline` or `Segments` collider is its edges (`ColliderGeometry::Edges`, world-space edges in the system's `edges` list, with their box for a quick reject): a particle meets an edge whose nearest point is within its radius, its normal the edge's turned to the side the particle is on (from the edge's end at an end), or an edge its move crossed this update, at the crossing, its normal to the side it came from (`edge_contact`; a crossing is met before any edge it merely ends near, the earliest first, else the nearest). A tile map's colliders are such edges, a chunk's outlines packed in one collider whose box is mostly air: by box, particles bounced off the air in it (`particles_meet_a_tile_part_s_edges_not_its_box`). The crossing keeps a fast particle from passing through an edge, which has no inside to catch it (`a_particle_crossing_an_edge_within_an_update_still_bounces`). Only a tile part's loops bound something solid: a `TilePart` that is not one-way, whose `Segments` (or, for a chunk of one loop, closed `Polyline`) physics winds clockwise on screen round its ground, holes included, so the ground is on the same side of every edge. They are stored turned round so each edge's left normal points out (unless the placement mirrors them), with `solid: Some(reach)`, `reach` half the cell of the `TileColliders` the part is a child of (`solid_contact`). A move into the ground across an edge is met where it crossed in; a move out is not met. Otherwise the part's nearest point to the particle decides, locally: within `reach` on its inner side (by the edge's normal, or at a corner the sum of its edges' normals) the particle is in the ground and goes out there, so landing dust spawned round the feet ends on top (`a_particle_starting_just_inside_tile_ground_ends_on_top_of_it`); on its outer side within the radius it touches. Local, because the loops round a place may lie in several parts: a room carved from a wall has its own loop, likely in another chunk's part, and a parity test over one part's edges judged every particle in such a room inside the wall mass and moved it to the mass's far edge each update (`a_particle_in_a_room_whose_wall_s_outline_lies_in_another_part_drifts_untouched`). The half-cell bound keeps a part's loop from claiming air a thinner wall away; ground deeper than that is not judged, and a particle in a thin floor leaves through the nearer face. A hole alone in its chunk, a closed polyline wound as a hole's, takes the tile rule too (`a_particle_in_a_room_alone_in_its_part_bounces_off_its_walls`). Every other collider's edges (a segment, any polyline, closed or not, a one-way run, a game's own `Segments`) are two-sided, as physics treats them: the side is the one the particle came from (`particles_stay_in_a_game_s_closed_polyline_arena_and_bounce`);
  - every other shape is its world bounds (`Collider::world_bounds`), a box.
- **Filtering** - a collider counts only if `collider.layer & config.collision_mask != 0`. A tile map storey's edge (the parts of a `TileColliders` whose crate-internal `surface` is false, as the map sets it on the grid that keeps characters on a raised floor; read in the same lookup as the part's cell) is no surface, and never counts (`a_raised_floor_s_edge_is_no_surface_for_particles`). The collider on a particle's own emitter entity is always ignored (particles spawn inside it). Each particle is a circle of `config.radius`. `Bounce` resolves every overlapping collider in turn (so a particle sliding on a floor still hits a wall gathered after it) but sends at most one event per update (a corner hit is one event); `Die`, `Stick` and `Event` stop at the first contact. Each collider is resolved once per update, so a particle squeezed between nearly opposing colliders can end an update overlapping one of them; the next update corrects it, and a gap narrower than the particle can't be resolved at all.
- **Responses** - `Die` removes the particle. `Bounce` reflects velocity along the contact normal with the restitution, then pushes the particle just outside the surface. `Stick` puts the particle on the surface, its radius out from the contact point along the normal (as `Bounce` places it: a fast particle that crossed an edge sticks where it crossed, not where its move ended inside, `a_fast_stick_particle_sticks_on_the_surface_it_crossed`), zeroes velocity and sets the private `stuck` flag: no more forces or movement, but it still ages, fades and dies. `Event` passes through.
- **Messages** - opt-in with `CollisionConfig::with_events()`: `Die` and `Stick` write one `ParticleCollision` message (read with `ctx.messages::<ParticleCollision>()`). `Bounce` emits only on contact entry (the particle touched no collider last update) and only when the impact speed along the normal is at least `MIN_BOUNCE_EVENT_SPEED` (20 px/s, also in the prelude). A particle resting on a surface stays touching, so it emits nothing more, whatever the gravity or dt. `CollisionResponse::Event` always emits on entry. Both entry checks use the private `touching` flag (not-touching to touching), which is per particle, not per collider: moving straight from one collider into an overlapping or adjacent one (or sliding along a floor into a wall) doesn't notify again. Events carry the emitter and collider entities, the particle position, the surface normal and the velocity before the response.
- **Cost** - brute force (every colliding particle against every gathered collider, and against every edge of an edges collider whose box its move reaches), which is fine for hundreds of particles against dozens of colliders. A tile map's chunk collider can hold hundreds of edges: particles over a busy map test them all.
- **Tunnelling** - collisions are checked at particle positions once per update, so very fast particles can pass through thin boxes and circles. Use thicker colliders or lower speeds. Edges also catch a move that crossed them.
